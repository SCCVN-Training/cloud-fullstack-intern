# Problem Statement

**Project Name:** SkillVerse – Community Skill Exchange

**Document Version:** 1.0

**Author:** Nhu Le Nguyen Quynh

**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026

---

# 1. Introduction

The rapid growth of online learning has made educational resources more accessible than ever. However, many existing platforms focus primarily on paid courses, subscriptions, or instructor-led training.

At the same time, millions of people possess valuable knowledge and practical skills that could benefit others but lack an easy and structured way to share them within their communities.

SkillVerse is proposed as a community-driven platform that enables individuals to exchange skills through collaboration rather than direct monetary payment.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Many people want to learn new skills but face several common challenges:

* High costs associated with online courses or private tutoring.
* Difficulty finding trustworthy people who can teach practical skills.
* Limited opportunities to exchange knowledge within local or online communities.
* Existing platforms mainly support one-way learning rather than mutual knowledge exchange.
* Individuals with valuable expertise often have no effective way to contribute and receive value in return.

As a result, many potential learning opportunities are lost because there is no structured system that encourages reciprocal teaching and learning.

---

# 3. Root Cause Analysis

The problem exists because:

* Knowledge is often treated as a commercial product rather than a community resource.
* Most learning platforms reward instructors financially instead of encouraging mutual collaboration.
* Community members with valuable experience lack a centralized platform to advertise and exchange their skills.
* Existing social media platforms facilitate communication but are not designed to organize structured skill exchange.

---

# 4. Impact of the Problem

### For Learners

* Limited access to affordable learning opportunities.
* Difficulty identifying suitable mentors.
* Reduced opportunities for practical, peer-to-peer learning.

### For Teachers

* Limited visibility for sharing personal expertise.
* Few incentives to volunteer knowledge.
* Difficulty connecting with interested learners.

### For Communities

* Valuable skills remain underutilized.
* Collaboration between community members is limited.
* Informal knowledge sharing is difficult to organize and sustain.

---

# 5. Existing Solutions

Current learning platforms provide valuable educational resources but primarily rely on financial transactions or traditional instructor-student models.

Examples include:

| Platform Type             | Strengths                       | Limitations                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Online learning platforms | High-quality structured courses | Often require payment and primarily support one-way learning  |
| Social media communities  | Easy communication              | No structured booking, reputation, or exchange system         |
| Community forums          | Knowledge sharing               | Limited support for organizing ongoing teaching relationships |

These solutions address parts of the problem but generally do not support reciprocal skill exchange through a virtual community reward system.

---

# 6. Proposed Solution

SkillVerse provides a web-based platform where users can exchange knowledge instead of money.

The platform enables users to:

* Create personal profiles.
* Publish skills they are willing to teach.
* Search for skills they want to learn.
* Request and manage learning sessions.
* Earn virtual Skill Coins by teaching.
* Spend Skill Coins to learn from others.
* Build trust through ratings and reviews.

This creates a sustainable learning ecosystem in which every participant can contribute knowledge while continuously developing new skills.

---

# 7. Value Proposition

SkillVerse delivers value by:

* Reducing financial barriers to learning.
* Encouraging peer-to-peer knowledge sharing.
* Rewarding community participation through Skill Coins.
* Supporting lifelong learning.
* Building stronger learning communities.

---

# 8. Target Users

The initial target audience includes:

* University students
* Recent graduates
* Working professionals
* Freelancers
* Hobbyists
* Community organizations

These users either possess valuable skills to share or seek affordable opportunities to acquire new ones.

---

# 9. Vision Statement

To build an accessible and collaborative learning platform where people empower one another by exchanging knowledge, developing practical skills, and strengthening communities through mutual teaching and learning.

---

# 10. Expected Outcomes

Upon completion of the project, SkillVerse is expected to:

* Enable users to exchange skills efficiently.
* Encourage community participation through a virtual reward system.
* Demonstrate a complete cloud-based full-stack application.
* Showcase modern software engineering practices following the Software Development Life Cycle.
* Serve as a scalable foundation for future enhancements, including real-time communication, AI-powered recommendations, and mobile support.

---

# 11. Summary

SkillVerse addresses the challenge of affordable and collaborative learning by creating a community-centered platform where individuals exchange knowledge instead of money.

By combining user-generated skill listings, structured learning sessions, a virtual credit system, and modern cloud technologies, the project aims to foster continuous learning while demonstrating professional full-stack software engineering practices.
